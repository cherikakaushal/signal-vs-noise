# What happens when context is missing?

## Idea

Instead of adding noise, I removed parts of the information to simulate incomplete data.

## Approach

- Removed key words from headlines  
- Measured similarity with original text  

## Observation

- Similarity dropped significantly  
- Some sentences lost most of their meaning  

## Insight

Missing context affects systems more severely than random noise.

## Conclusion

Incomplete information can break understanding faster than noisy data.
