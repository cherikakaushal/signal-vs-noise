# When does a system actually break?

## Idea

Instead of testing individual distortions, I combined noise, missing data, and bias.

## Approach

- Applied all three distortions together  
- Measured similarity with original text  

## Observation

- Similarity dropped sharply  
- Some samples lost most of their meaning  

## Insight

Systems do not fail because of one issue —  
they fail when multiple imperfections interact.

## Conclusion

Understanding combined effects is critical for building reliable systems.
