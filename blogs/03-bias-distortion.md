# What happens when information becomes biased?

## Idea

Instead of random noise or missing data, I introduced bias into the information.

## Approach

- Injected repeated bias-related words  
- Measured similarity with original text  

## Observation

- Meaning changed without being completely lost  
- Sentences were subtly distorted  

## Insight

Bias does not destroy information — it reshapes it.

## Conclusion

Distorted systems may appear stable while becoming misleading.
